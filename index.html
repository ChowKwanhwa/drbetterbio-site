Frequently Asked Questions

How do I build a routine with drbetterbio Skin?

Start simple: cleanser, one targeted serum, moisturizer and SPF in the morning. In the evening, swap SPF for a treatment such as Retinal Night Renewal. Add one new product at a time, two weeks apart.

Can I use vitamin C and retinal together?

Yes — just at different times. Vitamin C works best in the morning under sunscreen; retinal belongs in your evening routine.

Are the formulas suitable for sensitive skin?

All formulas are fragrance-free and dermatologist-tested. If your skin is reactive, patch-test first and introduce actives gradually.

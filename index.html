Frequently Asked Questions

Which products are available now?

Facial Sheet Mask, Eye Patches and Hydrating Peel-Off Mask are available to order today. Face Cream, Gentle Facial Cleanser and Facial Serum are coming soon.

How do I use the Facial Sheet Mask?

After cleansing, unfold the sheet and smooth it over your face. Relax for 10–15 minutes, remove, then gently pat any remaining essence into your skin.

How do I use the Hydrating Peel-Off Mask?

Apply an even layer to clean, dry skin with a mask brush, avoiding the eyes, brows and lips. Once it has set, peel it away from the edges and rinse off any residue.

How do I use the Eye Patches?

Place one patch under each eye on clean skin, leave on for 15–20 minutes, then remove. Ideal in the morning or before an evening out.

When will the new products launch?

Face Cream, Gentle Facial Cleanser and Facial Serum are on their way. Sign up to our newsletter below to be the first to know.

Are the products suitable for sensitive skin?

Please check the full ingredient list on each pack and patch-test any new product on a small area first, especially if your skin is reactive.

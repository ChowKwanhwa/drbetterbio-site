Frequently Asked Questions

Which products are available?

Facial Sheet Mask (25 sheets), Eye Patches (60 patches) and Hydrating Peel-Off Mask (150 mL) are all available to order today.

How do I use the Facial Sheet Mask?

After cleansing, unfold the crystal sheet and smooth it over your face so it sits closely against the skin. Leave on for 15–20 minutes, remove, then gently pat in any remaining essence. Each sheet is for single use.

How do I use the Hydrating Peel-Off Mask?

Apply an even layer to clean, dry skin, avoiding the eyes, brows and lips. Wait for it to set into a film, then gently peel it away to lift blackheads and dull surface build-up. Follow with the rest of your routine — skin is ready to absorb it.

How do I use the Eye Patches?

Take one patch for each eye from the jar and place it on clean skin around the eye area. Use daily as part of your routine to help smooth fine lines and keep the eye area looking firm and youthful.

Are the products suitable for sensitive skin?

Please check the full ingredient list on each pack and patch-test any new product on a small area first, especially if your skin is reactive.
